Privacy Policy

Pictures Plus · Effective date: November 9, 2026

Pictures Plus (“Pictures Plus,” “we,” “us”) is a custom framing and printing business in Hawaii. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you ask to receive text message notifications about your order at our Pictures Plus Kahala store, how we use it, and the choices you have. Information collected through our website, picturesplus.com, is described in the website Privacy Policy.

Information we collect

When you place a custom framing or print order at our store, you may choose to receive a text message about your order. If you do, we collect:

  • your name and your mobile phone number;
  • your work order number and order details needed to complete your order;
  • a record of your consent, which is the Text Message Notification Consent form you sign at the store, including the date you signed it.

How we use your information

We use your mobile number only to send you the text message you agreed to receive, which is a one-time message letting you know that your order is ready for pickup, and to keep a record of your consent. We do not use your mobile number for marketing or promotional text messages.

Text messaging (SMS)

Pictures Plus sends one text message per order, when your order is ready for pickup. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and you can reply HELP for help. After you reply STOP we will not send you further text messages unless you ask us to.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Who can see your information

We share your mobile number only with the service providers that help us deliver your text message, such as our text-message service provider and the wireless carriers that carry the message, and only as needed to deliver it. We may also disclose information if the law requires it. We do not sell your information.

How long we keep it

We keep your order records and signed consent form for as long as needed to run our business, answer questions about your order, and show that you agreed to receive the message. You may ask us to delete your mobile number at any time.

Your choices

  • Decline text messages when you place your order, and we will not text you.
  • Reply STOP to any text from us to opt out.
  • Email us to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it.

Security

We keep your information in our business systems and limit access to staff who need it to serve you. No method of storing or transmitting information is completely secure, but we work to protect it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
